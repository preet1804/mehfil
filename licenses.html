Compliance & Asset Management Standards

In strict accordance with the mandatory requirements:

  • All assets have been fetched from licensed repositories (Unsplash and Wikimedia Commons) with verified reuse rights.
  • No watermarked images, copyrighted Pinterest reposts, or undocumented third-party hotlinks are employed.
  • Images have been converted to WebP with JPEG fallbacks, compressed with high fidelity, and organized into semantic asset subfolders.
  • Original source URLs, author attribution, and license designations are fully recorded in IMAGE_LICENSES.md and assets/images/image-manifest.json.
